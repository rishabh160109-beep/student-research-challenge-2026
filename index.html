The project

Making AI work where
resources are limited.

Advanced language models require substantial memory and computing power. This research investigates whether 8-bit integer quantization can preserve useful model performance while significantly reducing deployment costs.

Why it matters

Efficient models can run on phones, laptops, and edge devices—bringing AI closer to people while reducing energy use and infrastructure demands.

The research question

How effectively can FP32 neural networks be converted to INT8 representations, and what trade-offs emerge in model accuracy and practical performance?
